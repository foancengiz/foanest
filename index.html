Who We Are

About
Foanest

Foanest is a real estate investment and development company focused on building sustainable, income-generating portfolios across the United States.

The company operates across multiple real estate strategies, including property acquisition, development, portfolio management, and structured investment models. Foanest aims to combine financial discipline with practical execution to create stable cash flow and long-term asset appreciation.

With a flexible and opportunity-driven approach, Foanest evaluates and executes projects across different asset types and markets, adapting to changing market conditions while maintaining a strong focus on value creation.
